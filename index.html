01 / RESEARCH + DATA
OMNI-REASON · DATA TEAM LEADVIDEO REASONING

Video-to-video reasoning

Building the data backbone for V2V research.

As Omni-Reason data team lead, I coordinated task design and dataset sourcing and developed TV2V/RV2V data workflows with shared task structure, human quality review, and traceable revisions. I also used training feedback and qualitative evaluation to help decide where more data was needed.

My role: Data team leadership, data infrastructure, task quality, and evaluation feedback.

Separate public research: I'm a co-author of the VBVR-Pro preprint. My contribution included data quality checks and qualitative model evaluation.

02 / AI + EVIDENCE
INDEPENDENT PROJECTPYTHON · COMPUTER VISION

SceneTrace

From video detections to a timeline you can inspect.

I built temporal event logic and spatial relationship analysis on top of a pretrained detector, then linked every observation to an evidence frame. The result helps a reviewer see what changed and check whether the visual evidence supports it.

What matters: It records observations, not ground truth. The included natural-motion review also documents an identity-tracking failure.

03 / 3D + INTERACTION
PERSONAL PRODUCT EXPERIMENTTHREE.JS · WEB

IRIS Motion Studio

Give a static 3D object a way to move.

What began as my exploration of a generated camera grew into a browser workbench: inspect model structure, define part motion, test it, and save a reusable recipe. I directed the product and original camera exploration; the web application was developed with Codex assistance.

What matters: It makes existing separate parts interactive. It does not automatically infer joints or turn a single mesh into an articulated model.

04 / DESIGN + PEOPLE
NO-WORRIES STUDENT PROJECTILLUSTRATION · PRODUCT DESIGN

Sparkle & Snuggle

From playful sketches to objects people can hold.

I led a four-person design team developing two original plush characters with interchangeable emotion clips, taking them from illustrations through prototypes and factory production. I also co-designed emotion-themed NFC keychains: a phone tap opens a short video our team made about handling that feeling.

My role: Character illustration, product details, prototype revisions, factory coordination, and keychain co-design. Videos and outreach were team efforts.

Whether I'm reviewing a model's evidence or revising a product prototype, I want the important decisions to be visible and the result to make sense to the person using it.
